📦 The <lazy> Built-in Element

Defer any inline markup declaratively — no separate component or registration options needed. Each block below is detached at parse time and inserted when its strategy fires.

<lazy>
👀 Inserted when scrolled into view
<lazy delay="2000">
⏱️ Inserted 2 seconds after mount
<lazy idle>
🧘 Inserted when the browser went idle
<lazy margin="100px"><heavy-chart /></lazy>
<lazy interaction="click,focus">…</lazy>
